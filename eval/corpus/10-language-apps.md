day 847 of the streak and this morning I couldn't order coffee in Lisbon. couldn't do it. the app says I'm 61% fluent and the actual Portuguese person just switched to English after watching me suffer for eight seconds. so what have I been doing for 847 days.

practicing the app, I think, is the honest answer. I've gotten really good at the app. multiple choice, word banks, the little green owl's specific idea of what a sentence is. none of which involves the actual skill, which is producing meaning under time pressure while someone looks at you. it's like I spent two years doing swimming flashcards.

but I don't want to fully trash it either, I do know a few thousand words I didn't know, that's not nothing, vocabulary has to come from somewhere. maybe the fair statement is the app is a good deck and a terrible coach, and the streak mechanic tricked me into confusing the deck for the sport. what would practicing the sport even look like at day one. embarrassment, probably. maybe the thing the app really sells is the avoidance of embarrassment, and embarrassment is the active ingredient.
