the city got louder this year, or I got more sensitive, hard to say which. construction on every block, trucks idling, someone's speaker on the train every single time now. I keep wondering if noise is a kind of rent we pay for living near each other. and whether quiet has quietly become a luxury good, the expensive neighborhoods are the silent ones, that can't be a coincidence.

my first instinct is regulation, decibel limits, actual enforcement. but then the loud street is also the alive street? the silence I want might just be the sound of a place dying, or of everyone poorer than me being priced out. is there a version of quiet that isn't just wealth. I don't know. maybe what I actually want is not silence but consent, some say in what enters my ears. that feels closer but I'm not sure what it implies.
