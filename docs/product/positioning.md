# gaddr — Positioning

**gaddr is a writing environment for people who write to figure out what they think — and it gives them a research-grade co-thinker without ever writing their prose.**

## Positioning statement

For **writers developing a stance** — essayists, researchers, students, anyone thinking on the page — who are stuck between *interruptive AI that drafts for them* and *a blank page with no help at all*, **gaddr** is a **thinking-first writing platform** that protects an uninterrupted freewrite and then meets the draft with a **constellation** of evidence, arguments, steelmanned counterarguments, citations, research directions, and questions. Unlike autocomplete assistants and chatbots, gaddr **never ghostwrites** — it sharpens the writer's own thinking instead of replacing it.

## The problem

Today's AI writing tools optimize the artifact, not the thinker. They draft, autocomplete, and flatter — lifting the page while homogenizing voice and quietly offloading the reasoning that makes writing worth doing. Grammar tools fix the surface. Neither helps you *develop a view you don't yet hold.*

## How it works

1. **Freewrite** — an uninterrupted sprint. No AI in the writing loop. You generate first.
2. **Constellation review** — after the timer, AI presents source-backed evidence, steelmanned counterarguments, citations, research directions, and generative questions as discrete, provenance-tagged nodes to react to.
3. **Final draft** — accepted findings become annotations that point, question, and explain; then you return to a clean surface to revise in your own words.

## What makes it different

- **Co-thinker, not ghostwriter.** AI may retrieve, structure, question, and annotate. It never composes the writer's sentences.
- **Co-thinker, not fact-checker.** The value is expanding and challenging your thinking — supplying the strongest opposing case a solitary draft always misses. Fact-checking is a quiet hygiene layer, never the headline.
- **The writer generates first.** The freewrite-first sequence keeps authorship — and the durable understanding — with the writer.
- **Provenance is always visible.** Every node is tagged sourced / inferred / heuristic, so evidence is never confused with the model's guess.

## Non-negotiables

- Writing surfaces stay interruption-free. Typing latency is P0.
- Counterarguments are steelmanned, not shallow or adversarial.
- Nothing auto-applies; the writer accepts, defers, or dismisses.
- Findings prompt reflection; they never hand over finished conclusions.

## What gaddr is not

An autocomplete engine. A chatbot that answers for you. A grammar checker. A citation police. A tool that makes your writing sound like everyone else's.
